    Why I made this

    I created Human Agency Check to explore a question I encounter in my own work: How do we gain the practical benefits of AI without quietly designing human judgment out of the process?

    I work across digital strategy, UX/UI, web development, and operations. In each discipline, adoption succeeds when technology works with the realities of the people using it. This early framework helps product teams, operators, and business leaders notice where an AI workflow supports people and where it may create dependence, obscure accountability, or remove meaningful choice.

    Working definition

    Human agency is a person's practical ability to understand, influence, question, correct, or reject a system's output while retaining responsibility for meaningful decisions.

    What this is not

    • A legal or compliance certification
    • A complete responsible-AI audit
    • A substitute for user research
