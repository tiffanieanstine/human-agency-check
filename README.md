# Human Agency Check
**[Try the live Human Agency Check](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tiffanieanstine/human-agency-check)**

An interactive framework for evaluating whether AI-enabled workflows preserve human judgment, agency, and accountability.

## The question behind the project

How do we gain the practical benefits of AI without quietly designing human judgment out of the process?

Human Agency Check evaluates a workflow across ten dimensions, including transparency, limitations, accountability, participation, control, uncertainty, meaningful work, explainability, privacy, and capability.

The goal is not less AI. It is more intentional AI.

## Working principle

> AI should remove unnecessary friction without removing human agency.

For this project, human agency means a person's practical ability to understand, influence, question, correct, or reject a system's output while retaining responsibility for meaningful decisions.

## What it returns

The assessment provides:

- an overall score;
- one of three directional result categories;
- the three lowest-scoring dimensions;
- practical recommendations for improving the workflow.

## Limitations

This is a conversation framework and an early prototype. It is not a legal or compliance certification, a complete responsible-AI audit, or a substitute for user research. The questions and thresholds should evolve through critique, testing, and interdisciplinary input.
